Safety Guidelines

Little Adventures Begin with Responsible Play

At Little Goonz, we believe movement, exploration, and play are important parts of childhood.

Whether your child is using a walker, enjoying their first scooter ride, learning to roller skate, or exploring skateboarding, following appropriate safety practices can help reduce avoidable risks.

These guidelines provide general safety information for Little Goonz products. They do not replace the instructions, warnings, weight limits, or other requirements supplied with each individual product.

1General Safety Guidelines

Before allowing a child to use any Little Goonz product, parents and caregivers should follow these precautions:

  • Always follow the manufacturer's instructions and product warnings.
  • Choose products appropriate for the child's age, size, abilities, and experience.
  • Provide adult supervision suitable for the child's age and activity.
  • Inspect products before each use.
  • Keep products away from traffic, stairs, steep slopes, and other hazards.
  • Use products only on surfaces suitable for their intended purpose.
  • Avoid use near swimming pools, open water, or unsafe elevated areas.
  • Use suitable protective equipment for riding and skating activities.
  • Do not use damaged, improperly assembled, or modified products.
  • Stop using a product if a component becomes loose, broken, or defective.

No mobility product or protective accessory can eliminate all risk of injury.

2Baby Walker Safety Guidelines

Applicable Products:

  • Teddy Trek 4-in-1
  • Joystep 4-in-1

Both products are listed in the Little Goonz catalog for children aged 6 months and above. Age eligibility alone does not establish that a particular child is developmentally ready for a product.

Recommended Precautions

Constant Adult Supervision

Never leave a child unattended while using a baby walker or any of its available modes.

Safe Indoor Environment

Keep walkers away from stairs, steps, uneven flooring, door thresholds, and areas where a child could fall or become trapped.

Remove Nearby Hazards

Keep hot liquids, electrical cords, sharp objects, and other potentially dangerous items out of the child's reach.

Correct Assembly

Ensure the seat, frame, handle, wheels, and activity components are installed and secured as instructed.

Appropriate Usage Modes

Teddy Trek and Joystep offer different movement and play functions. Use each mode only as directed in the product's specific instructions.

Avoid Unnecessary Extended Use

Follow the product's instructions for appropriate use and duration. A seated walker should not be treated as a substitute for natural floor-based movement or independent walking practice.

3Kids Scooter Safety Guidelines

Applicable Products:

  • Zippy DX Scooter
  • Zippy Kick Scooter

The catalog lists both scooters for children aged 2 years and above.

Recommended Precautions

Wear Appropriate Protection

Children should wear a properly fitted helmet and other suitable protective gear when riding.

Check the Scooter

Before use, inspect the handlebars, steering stem, wheels, deck, folding or locking mechanism, and brake.

Choose Suitable Riding Areas

Use scooters on smooth, dry, relatively level surfaces away from moving vehicles and hazardous obstacles.

Keep Riding Controlled

Children should ride at a manageable speed and avoid dangerous tricks, steep slopes, and crowded areas.

Adult Supervision

Younger children should always be closely supervised by a responsible adult.

Use LED Wheels Responsibly

LED wheels are decorative visibility features. They are not substitutes for suitable lighting, reflective equipment, or safe riding conditions.

4Roller Skate Safety Guidelines

Applicable Product: Hyper Speed Skates

Recommended catalog age: 6–14 years.

Roller skating requires balance, coordination, practice, and suitable equipment.

Recommended Precautions

  • Wear a properly fitted helmet and suitable wrist, elbow, and knee protection.
  • Check the skates' fit and make sure straps and fastening components are secure.
  • Inspect the wheels, frame, and moving parts before skating.
  • Begin practicing in a flat, open area away from vehicles.
  • Learn basic balance, turning, slowing, and stopping techniques before increasing speed.
  • Avoid wet, slippery, uneven, or debris-covered surfaces.
  • Do not skate while holding onto moving vehicles.
  • Stop using the skates if any component is loose, damaged, or functioning abnormally.

Children should be supervised according to their age, skating experience, and surroundings.

5Skateboard Safety Guidelines

Applicable Product: PP Skate Board

Recommended catalog age: Above 8 years.

Recommended Precautions

Use Protective Equipment

A suitable helmet, wrist guards, elbow pads, and knee pads are recommended.

Inspect Before Riding

Check the skateboard deck, wheels, trucks, and fasteners for damage or looseness.

Start with Basic Skills

Beginners should learn how to balance, push, turn, slow down, and step off safely before attempting more advanced movements.

Choose Safe Locations

Use smooth, open areas intended or suitable for skateboarding. Avoid traffic, steep slopes, and crowded public spaces.

Avoid Risky Stunts

Children should not attempt jumps or advanced tricks beyond their ability or without appropriate instruction and supervision.

Observe the Product's Limits

Always follow the weight, age, and usage restrictions supplied with the particular skateboard.

6Helmets & Protective Gear

Applicable Products:

  • Sports Helmet Pro
  • Sports Helmet Lite
  • Knee/Elbow/Wrist Guards

Protective equipment can help reduce certain injury risks, but no helmet or guard can prevent every injury.

Helmet Safety

  • Select a helmet suitable for the intended activity.
  • Follow the manufacturer's sizing and fitting instructions.
  • Ensure the helmet fits securely without excessive movement.
  • Fasten and adjust the chin strap as directed.
  • Inspect the shell, straps, and fastening components before use.
  • Replace a helmet after a significant impact or whenever replacement is required by its instructions.
  • Do not modify the helmet or use it if damaged.
  • Remove helmets when children are playing on playground equipment or in situations where straps could become caught.

Protective Guard Safety

  • Choose the correct size for the child.
  • Ensure the guards cover the intended areas.
  • Secure straps without restricting normal circulation or movement.
  • Inspect shells, padding, stitching, and fastening mechanisms before use.
  • Replace damaged or excessively worn protective equipment.

7Product Care & Storage

Appropriate product care helps maintain usability and allows problems to be identified before the next use.

  • Clean products according to their specific care instructions.
  • Dry products thoroughly after cleaning.
  • Avoid prolonged exposure to unsuitable heat or weather conditions.
  • Store products in a clean, dry place.
  • Inspect moving parts, wheels, and fasteners regularly.
  • Keep unused components and small parts away from young children.
  • Do not use unsuitable chemicals or attempt unauthorized repairs.

8What to Do If a Product Appears Unsafe

If you discover a defect or problem that may affect safe use:

  1. Stop using the product immediately.
  2. Keep the product away from children.
  3. Do not attempt to operate damaged moving or locking components.
  4. Record the product model and identification details.
  5. Contact the seller or Little Goonz support team for assistance.

If an injury occurs, prioritize appropriate medical attention.
