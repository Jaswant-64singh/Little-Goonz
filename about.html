ABOUT LITTLE GOONZ

Big Adventures for Little Explorers

At Little Goonz, we believe childhood is all about adventure, curiosity, and endless play. Every first step, first ride, first skate, and first roll is a new opportunity for children to explore the world around them. Our range of 4-in-1 walkers, kids' scooters, roller skates, skateboards, and protective gear is created to bring together playful design, durability, comfort, and the joy of movement.

We design products that are more than something to play with—they become companions as little explorers grow, learn balance, build confidence, and discover their independence. Crafted with care in India, Little Goonz aims to make every ride a little more exciting and every adventure a little more memorable.

Our Mission

To make childhood more joyful through products that inspire adventure, curiosity, movement, and playful discovery.

Our Vision

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Play Should Lead to Discovery

We believe children learn about the world by moving through it, trying new things, playing freely, and following their curiosity.

That’s why our approach brings together:

Adventure

Giving little explorers products that encourage them to move, ride, and discover.

Curiosity

Creating playful experiences that invite children to interact, explore, and try something new.

Confidence

Supporting the journey from first movements to new riding experiences.

Joy

Because childhood should have plenty of moments that simply feel fun.

These principles reflect the brand philosophy presented throughout the Little Goonz catalog.

A MESSAGE FROM OUR FOUNDER

Building Little Adventures That Grow With Them

“

I believe childhood is made up of little moments that become big memories a first step, a first ride, a first attempt, and the excitement of discovering something new.

At Little Goonz, our aim is to create products that become part of those moments. From playful walkers that accompany those early steps to scooters, skates, skateboards, and protective gear for bigger adventures, every product is inspired by the natural curiosity and energy of children.

We want Little Goonz to be more than a children's brand. We want it to be a part of their journey as they play, explore, gain confidence, and discover the world around them.

Our commitment is simple: to create playful, thoughtfully designed products that bring more joy to childhood and make every little adventure worth remembering.

Because every child deserves the freedom to explore, play, and grow—one little adventure at a time. ”
