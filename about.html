ABOUT LITTLE GOONZ

Big Adventures for Little Explorers

At Little Goonz, we believe childhood is all about adventure, curiosity, and endless play. Every first step, first ride, first skate, and first roll is a new opportunity for children to explore the world around them. Our range of 4-in-1 walkers, kids' scooters, roller skates, skateboards, and protective gear is created to bring together playful design, durability, comfort, and the joy of movement.

We design products that are more than something to play with—they become companions as little explorers grow, learn balance, build confidence, and discover their independence. Crafted with care in India, Little Goonz aims to make every ride a little more exciting and every adventure a little more memorable.

A MESSAGE FROM OUR FOUNDER

Building Little Adventures That Grow With Them

At Little Goonz, we believe childhood should be filled with joy, discovery, and endless possibilities. Every little adventure deserves a product that is thoughtfully designed, built to last, and made with care.

As a subsidiary of Bodygym, a trusted name in the fitness equipment industry for over 25 years, Little Goonz carries forward the same values of quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction that have been the foundation of our journey.

Our experience in manufacturing has taught us one simple principle: never compromise on quality. We bring this philosophy into every Little Goonz product, combining dependable manufacturing practices with innovative designs that cater to the evolving needs of Indian families.

Our vision is to continuously introduce new, practical, and exciting products to the Indian market products that make everyday life easier for parents and every adventure more enjoyable for children.

For us, customer satisfaction isn't just a goal; it's a commitment that drives everything we do. We take pride in building products that families can trust and in creating a brand that grows alongside their little ones.

With Little Goonz, we're not just making products. We're making little adventures possible.

Deepak Garg

Founder, Little Goonz
