1Introduction

Welcome to Little Goonz.

We respect the privacy of individuals who visit our website, contact our team, request product information, or enquire about dealership opportunities.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how it is used, when it may be shared, and how individuals can contact us about their information.

The website is operated by Your Full Name, operating under the Little Goonz brand, at C-599, DSIIDC industrial zone, Narela, Delhi-110040.

Throughout this Privacy Policy, "Little Goonz", "we", "our", and "us" refer to the website operator identified above.

By using our website, you can review our privacy practices. Where consent is required for particular processing activities, we will request it separately in accordance with applicable law.

2Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the website, we may collect the following information.

A. Contact Information

When you contact us, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Message or enquiry details
  • Other information you voluntarily provide

B. Dealer & Business Enquiry Information

When you submit a dealership enquiry, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Business or store name
  • Business email address
  • Contact number
  • City and state
  • Business type
  • Product interests
  • Information relevant to your dealership enquiry

We request that applicants provide only information reasonably necessary for their enquiry.

C. Product Support Information

If you contact us about a product, we may collect:

  • Product name or model
  • Description of the issue
  • Purchase details, where relevant
  • Photographs of the affected product
  • Details necessary to review the enquiry

Please do not submit sensitive personal information unless specifically requested and necessary.

D. Technical & Website Information

Depending on the website's technical configuration, information such as IP address, device and browser information, access logs, referring pages, and website interaction data may be processed.

3How We Use Your Information

We process personal information for purposes connected with operating the Little Goonz website and responding to legitimate enquiries.

These purposes may include:

Responding to Enquiries

Answering questions about Little Goonz products, features, and availability.

Dealer Enquiry Processing

Reviewing retailer and dealership enquiries and communicating about potential business opportunities.

Product Support

Reviewing product-related concerns and providing relevant assistance.

Website Administration

Maintaining website functionality, preventing misuse, and protecting the website's security.

Communication Preferences

Sending updates or promotional communications only where the required permission or another applicable lawful basis exists.

We will not use personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis.

4Dealer Enquiry Information

Information submitted through our Become a Dealer page is intended to help us understand the applicant's business and evaluate potential dealership opportunities.

Submission of an enquiry does not guarantee dealership approval or establish a dealer relationship.

Personal information submitted through the enquiry form will be handled in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

6Sharing of Personal Information

Personal information may be shared when reasonably necessary for legitimate website and business operations, including with:

  • Authorized employees or representatives handling enquiries
  • Website hosting and technical service providers
  • Service providers assisting with communication or website operations
  • Authorized business personnel handling dealer applications
  • Government or regulatory authorities when legally required

Any sharing must be consistent with the stated purposes, applicable contractual arrangements, and relevant legal requirements.

We will not disclose personal information for unrelated marketing purposes without the necessary lawful basis.

7Data Retention

At Little Goonz, we retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, maintain our business operations, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

The retention of personal information depends on its nature and purpose:

  • General Enquiries: Contact information and enquiry details are retained for the time needed to respond to questions, provide assistance, and complete any necessary follow-up.
  • Dealer Enquiries: Business and contact information submitted through our Become a Dealer form is retained while we review the enquiry, communicate with the applicant, and manage any resulting business relationship, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements.
  • Product Support Records: Information related to product enquiries, complaints, and support requests is retained as needed to resolve issues, provide follow-up assistance, and meet applicable legal obligations.
  • Website and Technical Logs: Technical information may be retained for website security, troubleshooting, performance monitoring, and compliance with applicable legal requirements.

We review our retention requirements and delete or anonymize personal information when it is no longer necessary, unless continued retention is required by applicable law or for the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims.

8Data Security

We recognize the importance of protecting personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, loss, and disclosure.

We implement appropriate technical and organizational safeguards based on the nature of the information processed and applicable legal requirements.

No internet-based transmission or storage method is completely free from security risks.

Individuals should avoid sending passwords, banking credentials, or unnecessary sensitive information through general enquiry forms.

9Children's Privacy

Little Goonz products are intended for children, but our website's contact, support, and dealership enquiry forms are intended for use by adults.

We do not seek to collect personal information directly from children through these forms.

Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of the website.

If we become aware that personal information about a child has been collected in circumstances that do not comply with applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

Any processing of children's personal information must comply with the applicable legal requirements concerning children and parental consent.

10Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have rights concerning their personal information, including rights to:

  • Request information about personal data processing
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate information
  • Request erasure where legally applicable
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Raise questions or grievances concerning the handling of personal information

Requests can be submitted to the privacy contact listed below.

We may need to verify the identity or authority of the requester before processing certain requests.

Applicable rights, procedures, and exceptions are governed by the laws in force at the relevant time.

11Third-Party Websites

The Little Goonz website may contain links to external websites, including social media platforms and third-party services.

We are not responsible for the independent privacy practices of external websites.

Visitors should review the privacy policies of any external services they choose to use.

12Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our website, business practices, or applicable legal requirements.

Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.

The latest version will be made available on this page with the corresponding revision date.
