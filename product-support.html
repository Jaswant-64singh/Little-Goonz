3Product Assembly & Maintenance

Proper assembly, routine inspection, and regular maintenance are important parts of responsible product use.

Before using a Little Goonz product:

  • Read the instructions and warnings supplied with the product.
  • Confirm that all required components are present.
  • Check that the product has been assembled correctly.
  • Inspect wheels, handles, fasteners, straps, and adjustable components where applicable.
  • Ensure moving and locking mechanisms function as described in the instructions.
  • Stop using the product if any component appears loose, broken, missing, or damaged.

Do not modify a product or substitute incompatible parts.

For model-specific instructions, contact our support team with the product name and details.

4Reporting a Product Issue

If you experience a problem with a Little Goonz product, please contact us with the following information:

  1. Product name and model
  2. Description of the issue
  3. Date and place of purchase, if available
  4. Purchase invoice or receipt, if available
  5. Product photographs showing the affected component
  6. Batch number or product identification details, if available

Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information or photographs of children.

Our team can review your enquiry and provide information about the appropriate next steps.

Important: If the issue involves a possible safety defect, stop using the product immediately until it has been appropriately assessed.
