Good to know

A few little answers

What ages are the walkers listed for?

Both Teddy Trek and Joystep are listed in the catalogue for ages 6 months and above.

How are Teddy Trek and Joystep different?

Teddy Trek offers walker, push-cart and stationary riding modes. Joystep offers rocker, walker and stationary modes, with an easy switch mechanism and a seat with a foot mat.

Can the music tray be removed?

Yes. Both walkers have detachable music trays and can be used as a food table when the tray is removed.
