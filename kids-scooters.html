Good to know

A few little answers

What ages are Zippy scooters listed for?

Both Zippy DX and Zippy Kick are listed for ages 2 years and above.

What is the difference between DX and Kick?

Zippy DX has all LED PU wheels, a play toy and 120-125 mm front wheels. Zippy Kick has front LED PU wheels and 120 mm front wheels.

Do both scooters fold?

Yes. The catalogue lists Japanese folding technology for both models, along with a twist-and-turn mechanism and a back brake with 60 mm wheels.
