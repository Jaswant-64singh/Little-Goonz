Little Goonz FAQs

Everything you need to know about Little Goonz products, safety, age suitability, and more.

Little Goonz offers a range of kids' mobility and adventure products, including 4-in-1 walkers, kids' scooters, roller skates, skateboards, sports helmets, and protective guards. Our products are designed to bring fun, movement, and excitement to little explorers.

Our products cater to different stages of childhood. Teddy Trek and Joystep walkers are listed for ages 6 months+, Zippy scooters for 2 years+, Hyper Speed Skates for 6–14 years, and the PP Skate Board for children above 8 years. Always check individual product age recommendations before use.

Little Goonz offers Teddy Trek 4-in-1 and Joystep 4-in-1 walkers for children aged 6 months and above. These products feature multiple modes and playful activities designed to make early movement and exploration more engaging.

Yes! Little Goonz offers Sports Helmet Pro, Sports Helmet Lite, and Knee/Elbow/Wrist Guards. These accessories complement activities such as scooting, skating, and skateboarding, helping families prepare for active outdoor play.
