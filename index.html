Little Goonz FAQs

Everything you need to know about Little Goonz products, safety, age suitability, and more.

Little Goonz offers a complete range of high-quality active play gear for children, including 4-in-1 baby activity walkers, 3-wheel and 2-wheel kick scooters, adjustable quad and inline roller skates, beginner skateboards, and premium certified safety gear.

Our products are thoughtfully tailored for children aged 6 months up to 12+ years. We offer baby walkers for infants (6–18 months), beginner 3-wheel scooters for toddlers (2–5 years), stunt and 2-wheel scooters for older kids (5–10 years), and adjustable skates and skateboards for ages 4 to 12+.

Our Teddy Trek 4-in-1 Baby Walkers and interactive activity walkers are specially built for babies aged 6 to 18 months. They feature cushioned ergonomic seats, wide anti-rollover safety bases, multi-stage push bars, and stimulating interactive toy trays with lights and music.

We offer lean-to-steer 3-wheel scooters with light-up LED PU wheels for toddlers and beginners, as well as foldable, height-adjustable 2-wheel scooters with rear fender brakes for growing adventurers seeking speed and agility.

Yes! Safety is our top priority. We offer multi-sport impact-resistant helmets with EPS foam padding and dial-fit systems, along with 6-piece protective pad sets (knee pads, elbow pads, and wrist guards) designed specifically for young riders.

Little Goonz offers 4-size adjustable quad skates and inline skates equipped with smooth ABEC-7 bearings, illuminated LED wheels, secure triple-lock buckle systems, and breathable inner liners for all-day comfort and stability.

We offer 22-inch mini cruiser skateboards and 28-inch double-kick maple skateboards with anti-slip grip tape, heavy-duty aluminum trucks, and soft shock-absorbing PU wheels ideal for kids learning balance and basic tricks.

Little Goonz products are proudly engineered and assembled in certified, world-class manufacturing facilities adhering to strict BIS and international safety standards (EN71, ASTM). Every product undergoes rigorous drop, impact, and non-toxic material testing before reaching your family.
